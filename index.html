Gerrit Wilkens

DNA nanotechnology | Self Assembly



I am interested in how programmable biomolecular systems can be designed to assemble, interact, and perform useful functions. My research focuses on DNA nanotechnology and particularly DNA origami.


I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Hybrid Nanostructures Group led by Prof. Ilko Bald at the University of Potsdam.


My current work is supported by a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Postdoctoral Fellowship.
