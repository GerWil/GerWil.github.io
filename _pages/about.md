I am interested in how programmable biomolecular systems can be designed
to assemble, interact, and perform useful functions. My research focuses on DNA nanotechnology and particularly DNA origami.
<br>
<br>
<br>
I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Hybrid Nanostructures Group
led by Prof. Ilko Bald at the University of Potsdam.
<br>
<br>
<br>
My current work is supported by a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Postdoctoral Fellowship.
